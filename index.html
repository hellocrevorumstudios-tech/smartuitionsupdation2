Hello, everyone! I’m Nithin, the founder of Crevorum Studios and NodeLit. I’m a ninth-grade student at The PSBB Millennium School, Coimbatore, and I developed this website. I’d like to share how this project began.

I initially created this website as a draft project. One day, I brought my laptop to tuition and showed the website to Mr.Antony sir. Managing my time was challenging, as I had to balance my schoolwork with my evening tuition commitments. However, being a night owl, I often preferred working on my projects at night.
